### Persistence and checklist format

LocalStorage key: `taskflow-board`. Its unversioned JSON contains the entire Board: `id`, `name`, `lastUpdated`, and `columns[]`; each column has `id`, `title`, and `tasks[]`. Tasks contain `id`, `title`, optional `description`, `priority`, `category`, `status`, `createdAt`, and embedded `subtasks[]`. Each checklist item contains only `id`, `title`, and `completed`.

Dates serialize as strings. Loading restores dates and defaults missing `lastUpdated` and `subtasks`; it does not validate the complete structure or restore missing workflow columns. The hook loads on every render and writes the complete board in an effect when board state changes. Storage reads/writes are synchronous; read access is outside the parsing catch, and writes have no catch. No storage-event or conflict policy exists.

Checklist completion is independent of parent status. Checklist items are not draggable workflow tasks. Item deletion removes that embedded record; deleting its task removes its checklist. Legacy checklist migration therefore needs approved defaults for the fields that full child tasks require.

## 7. Scope mapping

### Keep in v1.5

Reliability, runtime storage validation, safe recovery, supported migrations, a simple multiple-tab policy, strict TypeScript, reducer integrity, typed notifications, dialog/keyboard accessibility, targeted responsive/CSS corrections, automated tests, CI, documentation, and release confidence. Introduce tests before high-risk state and migration changes. Preserve the existing visual identity and reducer architecture.

Parent-child tasks remain in v1.5 with one-level relationships. Established scope: tasks may have no parent; a child cannot also be a parent; new children begin in To Do and move independently; parents cannot reach Done until all children are Done; related tasks may occupy different columns. Parent deletion requires relationship-aware confirmation and must not silently delete children. Children may be detached or reassigned. Search/filters evaluate tasks independently; cards communicate relationships when related tasks are hidden. Legacy checklist conversion uses explicit, repeat-safe defaults. [#60](https://github.com/garrib10/taskflow/issues/60) owns the complete decision record; this summary does not replace it or choose migration defaults.

### Save for v2.0

> TaskFlow v1.5 does not introduce column-capacity limits or WIP enforcement. Capacity design and implementation are deferred to the v2.0 board redesign.

Save this for v2.0: major visual redesign, a new application shell/information architecture, dedicated task-detail redesign, broad board interaction/mobile navigation changes, manual ordering redesign, and large state-architecture replacement. Minimal child management in v1.5 must not expand into the v2.0 detail redesign.

Backend, authentication, collaboration, SaaS, and AI work are outside v1.5 and are not proposed for v2.0 by this audit.

### Leave unscheduled

Uncommitted productivity features advertised by the stale README, including analytics, metrics, due dates, history/audit logs, assignees, and notes. Import/export notifications are not part of the committed v1.5 work. Do not create new issues or expand notification work to introduce these features.
